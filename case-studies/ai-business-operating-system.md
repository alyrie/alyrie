# AI Business Operating System

**Multi-agent workflow architecture for moving ideas from research to execution**

## The problem

AI is very good at producing individual outputs. That does not automatically make it good at running a durable business process.

I wanted a system that could move an idea through research, evaluation, planning, implementation, review, and documentation without turning into a pile of disconnected chats and forgotten decisions.

## What I designed

I built an operating model around specialized AI responsibilities rather than one general-purpose assistant.

The system separates work into functions such as:

- Opportunity and market research
- Reality checks and evidence review
- IP and brand screening
- Brand exploration
- Product and implementation planning
- Security review
- Revenue experimentation
- Documentation and decision capture

The agents are only one layer. The larger system defines how work moves between them, what evidence should survive a handoff, where human decisions are required, and how completed research becomes durable organizational knowledge.

## Architecture principles

### Specialized responsibility
Each workflow has a narrow job and a defined output instead of asking one AI conversation to be researcher, strategist, builder, critic, and record keeper simultaneously.

### Evidence before momentum
Research and validation are designed to challenge an idea before implementation effort compounds around an unsupported assumption.

### Human decision gates
Automation should reduce repetitive work without quietly making consequential product or business decisions on its own.

### Durable state
Important findings, decisions, status, and outputs are captured outside the conversational layer so the system can continue across sessions and tools.

### Orchestration over prompting
The value of the system comes primarily from responsibilities, sequencing, handoffs, review gates, and information architecture rather than a collection of clever prompts.
