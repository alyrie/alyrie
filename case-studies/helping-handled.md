# Helping Handled

**Recipient-led coordination platform**

## The problem

When someone is going through a difficult season, people around them often genuinely want to help. The coordination required to turn those offers into useful action can become another job for the person who already has too much to manage.

Helping Handled approaches that problem from the recipient's point of view. The product is designed to reduce the work of asking, coordinating, explaining, and keeping track of practical support.

## Product principles

The system is built around a few deliberate choices:

1. **Recipient first.** The person receiving help should not become the project manager for everyone offering it.
2. **Low-friction interaction.** Common actions should require as little explanation and navigation as possible.
3. **Clear ownership.** The system should make it obvious when something has been shared, claimed, or handled.
4. **Privacy by design.** A support network can involve sensitive personal information, so access and visibility are product decisions rather than afterthoughts.
5. **Coordination without noise.** More communication is not automatically better. The product should surface the information people need to act.
